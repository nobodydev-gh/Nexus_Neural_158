# Algorithms

- **Blur** — variance of Laplacian.
- **FAST-9 + BRIEF-128** — corners and Hamming matching with a ratio test.
- **Triangulation** — midpoint of two camera rays from known poses.
- **PatchMatch MVS** — depth hypotheses along the ray, NCC vs source views, spatial refine.
- **Umeyama Sim(3)** — 3×3 Jacobi SVD.
- **Geodesy** — WGS84 ellipsoid, ECEF, ENU.
- **Confidence** — weighted mix of observations, angle diversity, reprojection, depth consistency, density, image quality, (1 − dynamic probability), visibility.
- **Measurement** — Euclidean / horizontal / vertical; shoelace area; prism volume. Worst nearby confidence class emits a warning.
